DROP TEAM LOGOS HERE
====================

Two ways to get logos in. Both work.

1) THIS FOLDER  — logos here ship with the site when you deploy it, so everyone
   who opens your link sees them.

   Name each file after the team id:

       ohiostate.png
       texasam.png
       olemiss.svg
       boisestate.webp

   Supported: .png  .svg  .webp  .jpg  .jpeg
   Transparent PNG or SVG looks best — the logo sits on the team's primary colour.

2) THE APP  — Committee Room -> Logos -> drag in a whole folder at once. Filenames
   are matched automatically and it's forgiving:

       Ohio_State_Buckeyes_logo.png   -> ohiostate
       Ohio State.svg                 -> ohiostate
       OSU.png                        -> ohiostate
       notre-dame-fighting-irish.png  -> notredame

   It reports any file it couldn't place. These are stored on your device only,
   so use option 1 if you want your league to see them too.

No logo at all? The team falls back to a colour plate with its wordmark, which
still looks right on the broadcast.

Team ids are the first column in js/teams.js. Common ones:

    alabama        georgia        ohiostate      michigan       oregon
    texas          texasam        olemiss        notredame      pennstate
    tennessee      lsu            oklahoma       clemson        miami
    boisestate     smu            indiana        arizonastate   texastech

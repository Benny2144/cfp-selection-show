Your league data, explained

PRIVACY
WITHOUT MYSTERY.

CFP Dynasty Studio only collects the information needed to identify you, save your commissioner workspace, and restore it on another device.

Effective August 1, 2026

WHAT WE COLLECT

If you choose Google sign-in, Google provides your unique account identifier, verified email address, display name, and profile picture. We do not receive or store your Google password, Gmail messages, contacts, files, or calendar. The studio also stores the content you create: playoff field, league identity, scores, show settings, custom teams, pick'em entries, dynasty history, and uploaded team logos.

WHERE IT LIVES

Your working copy is stored in your browser for fast and offline use. When signed in, workspace data and account records are stored in Cloudflare D1; uploaded logos are stored privately in Cloudflare R2. Login sessions use a random token in a secure, HttpOnly cookie. Only a one-way hash of that token is stored.

HOW WE USE IT

We use this data only to authenticate you, save and restore your studio, prevent conflicting devices from silently overwriting each other, operate the service, and investigate security or reliability issues. We do not sell personal information or use your workspace for advertising.

SHARING & LINKS

We do not make signed-in account data public. If you create a league share link, the field configuration needed for the viewer experience is encoded in that link. Anyone who receives it may open the show, so treat league links as shareable invitations and avoid putting sensitive information in team or league names.

RETENTION & CONTROL

Cloud data remains until you delete your account or the service is discontinued. Use Save & sign in → Delete cloud account to delete your account record, saved workspace, active sessions, and private uploaded logos. A local browser copy is intentionally preserved so an accidental cloud deletion does not destroy your board; you may clear it through the studio or your browser's site-data controls.

SECURITY

Traffic is protected with HTTPS. Google identity tokens are verified on the server. Account mutations require a same-origin request, session cookies are Secure and SameSite, stored snapshots are size- and shape-validated, and uploaded logo formats and sizes are restricted. No online system can promise absolute security.

CHILDREN & CHANGES

The service is not directed to children under 13. We may update this policy as the product changes; the effective date above will be revised when material changes are published.

Your league data, explained

PRIVACY
WITHOUT MYSTERY.

CFP Dynasty Studio only collects the information needed to identify you, save your commissioner workspace, and restore it on another device.

Effective August 1, 2026

WHAT WE COLLECT

If you choose Google sign-in, Google provides your unique account identifier, verified email address, display name, and profile picture. We do not receive or store your Google password, Gmail messages, contacts, files, or calendar. The studio also stores the content you create: playoff field, league identity, scores, show settings, custom teams, pick'em entries, dynasty history, published Selection Night events, league-room memberships and activity, and uploaded team logos.

WHERE IT LIVES

Your working copy is stored in your browser for fast and offline use. When signed in, workspace data and account records are stored in Cloudflare D1; uploaded logos are stored privately in Cloudflare R2. Login sessions use a random token in a secure, HttpOnly cookie. Only a one-way hash of that token is stored.

HOW WE USE IT

We use this data only to authenticate you, save and restore your studio, prevent conflicting devices from silently overwriting each other, operate the service, limit abusive sign-in and write traffic, and investigate security or reliability issues. Cloudflare rate-limit counters use a short-lived request key and are not added to your profile or workspace. We do not sell personal information or use your workspace for advertising.

SHARING & LINKS

We do not expose your email, account profile, private workspace history, predictions, or unpublished boards through a public event. When you publish a Selection Night event, the broadcast field and presentation settings are stored under a short public link so anyone who receives it can watch. The service records an aggregate open count; a first-party cookie avoids counting the same browser again for twelve hours. Portable preview links instead carry the field configuration inside the URL. League-room invitations require sign-in; room members can see other members' display names, profile pictures, roles, shared board, room activity, and whether a member is currently connected to the live room, but not their email addresses or private account workspaces. Live presence exists only for the WebSocket connection and is not added to room history. Treat links as shareable invitations and avoid sensitive information in team or league names.

RETENTION & CONTROL

Cloud data remains until you delete it, delete your account, or the service is discontinued. You can revoke an individual published event, leave a league room, or—if you own it—delete the room and its shared board. Use Save & sign in → Delete cloud account to delete your account record, owned rooms, memberships in other rooms, published events, saved workspace, active sessions, and private uploaded logos. A local browser copy is intentionally preserved so an accidental cloud deletion does not destroy your board; you may clear it through the studio or your browser's site-data controls.

DATA PORTABILITY

Signed-in users can choose Save & sign in → Account Control → Download my data to receive a JSON export containing their profile, saved workspace, published broadcasts, league memberships, uploaded-logo metadata, and recent security activity. Session secrets and Google provider identifiers are excluded from the export.

SECURITY

Traffic is protected with HTTPS. Google identity tokens are verified on the server. Account mutations require a same-origin request, Cloudflare-native limits restrict repeated sign-in and write attempts, session cookies are Secure and SameSite, stored snapshots are size- and shape-validated, and uploaded logo formats and sizes are restricted. From the account center, you can sign out every other active session while keeping the current device connected. No online system can promise absolute security.

CHILDREN & CHANGES

The service is not directed to children under 13. We may update this policy as the product changes; the effective date above will be revised when material changes are published.

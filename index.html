Two audio files drive the show: the spoken intro during the cold open, then the music bed under the picks. Swap them by replacing the mp3s in this folder and updating INTRO_FILE / MUSIC_FILE at the top of js/show.js.

HOW IT WORKS

  1. Build the field. Click teams to fill seeds 1 through 12, drag to reorder, type in records. Only you see it.
  2. Set the show. Reveal order, pace, cold open, effects, music.
  3. Go live. Get the league on a stream, go fullscreen, hit play. The intro package rolls, then the picks drop one at a time.
  4. Send the bracket. Copy Share Link puts the whole field in a URL — whoever opens it lands on the play button with your bracket loaded.

Nothing about your field — not even the ticker — shows up on screen before you reveal it.
